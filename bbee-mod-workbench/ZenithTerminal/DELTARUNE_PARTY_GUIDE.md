# DELTARUNE: Party 0.6.4 (alpha test), in The Zenith Terminal

Kris from DELTARUNE as a playable character in BlazBlue Entropy Effect, with Susie and Ralsei fighting beside them. It is part of Cross Effect, one of the Redux mods. It comes with the Terminal, switched on, for game build **25627491**.

This is an unofficial fan-made mod. It isn't made, endorsed or supported by 91Act, Arc System Works, Toby Fox or the DELTARUNE team. Play single player only.

**You don't need DELTARUNE.** This is an alpha test, and the Terminal carries the DELTARUNE files the three are built from, in `Launcher\mods\dr-party\pack`: the sprites the mod uses, their sounds, the font and the portrait artwork. They belong to DELTARUNE's owners. Please don't pass that folder on by itself. A later release is to read the same files from your own copy of DELTARUNE instead. If you like these three, play DELTARUNE.

## Getting Kris into the game

Press the Terminal's large button (SET UP AND INSTALL, INSTALL or APPLY CHANGES). Kris is at the end of the Prototype list.

To leave Kris out, switch **DELTARUNE: Party** off in the Library and press the large button. Taking a character out removes that character's Evotypes, records and a run in progress from your save. The Terminal copies your whole save first.

## How to play

The buttons are the game's own, so they follow your settings.

- **Attack:** Kris's sword string on the ground, a slash and a spin in the air.
- **Hold Skill: the party command.** Time slows and DELTARUNE's target screen opens. Move to pick an enemy, then let go: Susie throws **Rude Buster** at it. Press **Dash** while you hold Skill to swap to Ralsei, who casts **Fireshock**. It costs no MP: Susie and Ralsei each recharge for a few seconds (the tabs above your HP bar show it).
- **TP:** perfect dodges fill the Tension meter under your HP bar. It raises your damage, up to +20% at MAX.
- **Potentials:** 36 cards in the usual reward choices. Among them X Slash (hold Attack), Party Dash (all three dash together), and the Pen of Hope (Summon switches Auto on: Susie and Ralsei act on their own).
- **Down + Summon:** a little dance.

Susie and Ralsei follow you, can't be hurt and don't need looking after.

## Known issues

This is an alpha test. It is playable from the lobby through a run, and unfinished in the ways listed here.

- **English text only.** The mod's names and descriptions are English in every game language.
- **No Legacy skill yet.** Kris's Legacy slots are empty, on the Evotype screens and in the Prototype File.
- **Stand-in icons.** Kris's talent (Second Wind) uses another character's glyph, and Potential Unleashed uses the game's own crest.
- **First-pass effects.** Kris's slashes and Rude Buster's chain are not the final effects. Party Dash has no platforms yet.
- **3 of the 36 Potentials are only partly done.** Their cards can be picked; part of what the text says may be missing.
- **Tested on one screen shape.** Everything was checked at 3440 x 1440. At 16:9 the party tabs and the Tension meter should follow the HP bar, but that hasn't been seen.
- **For the first half second of a stage** the party tabs and the Tension meter can sit slightly off before they snap to the HP bar.
- **Not seen yet by us:** a boss fight with the party, the run's result screen, and a revive with one left (Kris's SOUL should shatter and form again).
- **Not played yet from a Terminal install.** The mod is the same one as in its own package. Built and installed by the Terminal, it has been checked up to the install, not in a run.
- **Single player only.** Co-op and the online modes were not tested and are not supported.
- Kris's portraits are DELTARUNE's official artwork, placed to stand at the height of the game's own cast; they are not drawn in this game's style.

## What we would like you to try

1. **The party command** (hold Skill): does picking a target feel quick enough in a busy fight? Do Rude Buster and Fireshock hit what you picked?
2. **A whole run** on the Entropy you normally play: is Kris too weak or too strong against the characters you know?
3. **Potentials:** which ones did you take, which felt useless, which felt broken?
4. **Take a fatal hit with a revive left**, and tell us what you saw.
5. **Anything on the screen that sits over something else**, or that stays when it shouldn't (after a stage, in menus, on the result screen).

## If something goes wrong

- **Kris or the party have no sound.** The sounds are loaded from the Terminal's folder. If it was moved or renamed after the install, put it back where it was.
- **The game says the save is corrupted, or won't load.** Close the game without choosing anything that resets the save. Then use **Options > Saves and reports > REPAIR** in the Terminal, or install again.
- **To send a report:** **Options > Saves and reports > MAKE REPORT**. The zip holds the mods' log, where lines with `[DR-PARTY]` are this mod's. With it, say the version (0.6.4), the Entropy you played at and which Potentials you had.
